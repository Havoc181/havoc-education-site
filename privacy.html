Privacy

Paramedic VR Simulator and this website. Last updated 20 November 2026.

The short version

The simulator does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it works without an internet connection.

The VR app

  • Everything runs on the headset. Nothing about you or what you do in a scenario is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your comfort settings (movement, turning, seated mode) are saved on the headset only.
  • The app uses the local network only for the optional instructor link described below. It does not use the internet.

The instructor link (optional)

If an instructor connects a laptop to the headset over a local network, the headset sends that laptop what happens in the scenario (questions asked, findings checked, position in the room) so the instructor can follow along. This stays between the two devices. If the instructor saves a session log or notes, they are stored on that laptop only, under the control of the instructor and their organisation.

Meta

The app runs on Meta Quest headsets. Meta's own handling of your headset and account is covered by Meta's privacy policy, not this one.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which process standard connection information (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
